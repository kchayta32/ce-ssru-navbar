		<h1 class="ce-news-title">CE SSRU Faculty and Students Win "Good" Oral Presentation Award at the 25th GNRU National Research Conference</h1>

	</header>

	<div class="ce-news-body">
		<p class="ce-news-lead">The Department of Computer Engineering congratulates <strong>Asst. Prof. Dr. Rawi Uttamathanin</strong> (faculty member, Department of Computer Engineering), <strong>Miss Jidapa Jui-iam</strong>, <strong>Miss Wassana Pornprapha</strong>, and <strong>Miss Yanisa Muangsee</strong>.</p>

		<div class="ce-news-callout">
			<h2 class="ce-news-callout-title ce-emo-before ce-emo-trophy">Research &amp; Award Details</h2>

			<ul class="ce-news-list">
				<li><strong>Research Title:</strong> Wheatgrass Care Cabinet via Pet Application</li>
				<li><strong>Award Received:</strong> Good Oral Presentation Award</li>
				<li><strong>Conference:</strong> The 25th Graduate Network of Northern Rajabhat Universities (GNRU) National Research Conference — "AI: A New Dimension of Education for the Future"</li>
			</ul>
		</div>

		<p>Held on July 26, 2025 at Pibulsongkram Rajabhat University, the conference was organized by the Educational Administration Professional Development Association of Thailand, the Social Science Research Association of Thailand, and the Association of Technology and Innovation Professionals for Local Development in collaboration with all 8 member institutions of the Graduate Network of Northern Rajabhat Universities (GNRU).</p>
