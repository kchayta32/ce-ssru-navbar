		<h1 class="ce-news-title">CE SSRU Students and Faculty Win AI Innovation, Graceful AI Innovator, and Super Mentor Awards</h1>

		<p class="ce-news-subtitle ce-emo-before ce-emo-pin">AI Innovator Incubation Unit Project at Thepsatri Rajabhat University, Lopburi</p>
	</header>

	<div class="ce-news-body">
		<p class="ce-news-lead">The Department of Computer Engineering congratulates our students and faculty members on receiving the following awards:</p>

		<div class="ce-news-callout">
			<h2 class="ce-news-callout-title ce-emo-before ce-emo-medal">Award Recipients</h2>

			<ol class="ce-news-award-list">
				<li><strong>1. Mr. Kitti Chayta</strong> (Student ID: 66122519032) — <strong>AI Innovation Award</strong> (Best AI Innovation, THB 5,000 prize) and <strong>Graceful AI Innovator Award</strong> (THB 1,000 prize)</li>
				<li><strong>2. Mr. Sattawat Intharak</strong> (Student ID: 66122519012) — <strong>Graceful AI Innovator Award</strong> (THB 1,000 prize)</li>
				<li><strong>3. Miss Thanaphon Wiratmongkhonchai</strong> (Student ID: 66122519084) — <strong>Graceful AI Innovator Award</strong> (THB 1,000 prize)</li>
				<li><strong>4. Mr. Aphichai Praman</strong> (Student ID: 66122519067) — <strong>Graceful AI Innovator Award</strong> (THB 1,000 prize)</li>
				<li><strong>5. Mr. Saknarin Srichan</strong> (Student ID: 66122519047) — <strong>Graceful AI Innovator Award</strong> (THB 1,000 prize)</li>
				<li><strong>6. Lecturer Kan Charoenchit</strong> (Faculty Member, Department of Computer Engineering) — Honored with the <strong>"Super Mentor"</strong> Award</li>
				<li><strong>7. Asst. Prof. Dr. Sethakarn Prongnuch</strong> (Head of the Department of Robotics Engineering) — Congratulations to <strong>Team 4Guys</strong> for their project "Facial Recognition Student Attendance System," which earned an <strong>Honorable Mention in AI Innovation</strong> under his mentorship</li>
			</ol>
		</div>

		<p>The event was held under the AI Innovator Incubation Unit Project at Thepsatri Rajabhat University, Lopburi Province, in collaboration with the Artificial Intelligence Association of Thailand (AIAT), the Program Management Unit for Human Resources &amp; Institutional Development, Research and Innovation (PMU-B), the Ministry of Higher Education, Science, Research and Innovation (MHESI), the National Science and Technology Development Agency (NSTDA), and Rajamangala University of Technology Thanyaburi (RMUTT).</p>

		<p class="ce-emo-before ce-emo-spark">We commend your capability, creativity, and vision in applying artificial intelligence technologies to benefit society—a proud achievement for the Department of Computer Engineering.</p>
