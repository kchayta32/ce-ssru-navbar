International Exchange Program November 18, 2026

CE SSRU Students Participate in the KAIT Technical Internship Program (Fall 2026) in Japan

Kanagawa Institute of Technology, Japan (November 11 – December 21, 2026)

Students from the Faculty of Engineering and Industrial Technology, Suan Sunandha Rajabhat University, joined an international academic exchange workshop with Kanagawa Institute of Technology (KAIT), Japan, under the KAIT Technical Internship Program (Fall 2026).

Program Delegation & Details

  • Supervising Faculty Member: Asst. Prof. Dr. Kraiop Charoensopa
  • Computer Engineering Students:
    • Miss Anantaya Chancharoen
    • Miss Thanaphon Wiratmongkhonchai
    • Miss Kesinee Saesantia
  • Program Duration: November 11 – December 21, 2026
  • Objectives: To promote cultural exchange, teaching and learning collaboration, and hands-on professional internship experience
