		<h1 class="ce-news-title">Volunteer Academic Service Project: "Painting Colors, Filling Dreams for Kids" at Hua Khao Kaeo School, Nakhon Nayok</h1>

		<p class="ce-news-subtitle ce-emo-before ce-emo-pin">August 21–22, 2026 — By 2nd–4th Year Students and Faculty Members, Department of Computer Engineering</p>
	</header>

	<div class="ce-news-body">
		<p class="ce-news-lead">On August 21–22, 2026, second- to fourth-year students and faculty members of the Department of Computer Engineering organized the volunteer academic service project "Painting Colors, Filling Dreams for Kids" at Hua Khao Kaeo School in Nakhon Nayok Province.</p>

		<div class="ce-news-callout">
			<h2 class="ce-news-callout-title ce-emo-before ce-emo-target">Project Objectives</h2>

			<ol class="ce-news-list">
				<li>Repair, restore, and upgrade malfunctioning computers</li>
				<li>Repaint school buildings and mark sports court lines</li>
				<li>Improve the surrounding school landscape</li>
			</ol>
		</div>

		<p class="ce-emo-before ce-emo-balloon">The camp also strengthened unity, collaborative planning, and social responsibility while giving students an opportunity to apply their technical knowledge for community benefit.</p>

		<p class="ce-emo-before ce-emo-trophy">Computer equipment was also donated to the school to support ongoing educational activities.</p>
	</div>
