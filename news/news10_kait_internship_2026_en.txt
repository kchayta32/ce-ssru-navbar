		<p class="ce-news-lead">Students from the Faculty of Engineering and Industrial Technology, Suan Sunandha Rajabhat University, joined an international academic exchange workshop with <strong>Kanagawa Institute of Technology (KAIT), Japan</strong>, under the <strong>KAIT Technical Internship Program (Fall 2026)</strong>.</p>

		<div class="ce-news-callout">
			<h2 class="ce-news-callout-title ce-emo-before ce-emo-target">Program Delegation &amp; Details</h2>

						<li>Miss Thanaphon Wiratmongkhonchai</li>
						<li>Miss Kesinee Saesantia</li>
					</ul>
				</li>
				<li><strong>Program Duration:</strong> November 11 – December 21, 2026</li>
				<li><strong>Objectives:</strong> To promote cultural exchange, teaching and learning collaboration, and hands-on professional internship experience</li>
			</ul>
